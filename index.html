Linear Reveal

A clean, continuous sweep across any axis. Choose from 6 directions: Front-to-Back, Back-to-Front, Left-to-Right, Right-to-Left, Top-to-Bottom, or Bottom-to-Top.

GIF preview
✨

Segmentic Reveal

The section box expands outward from the model center in layered segments, creating a dramatic, multi-dimensional architectural reveal effect.

▶
GIF preview
🔄

Periodic Reveal

A rhythmic pulsing oscillation — the section box breathes in and out. Ideal for looping GIFs and social media content that demands attention.

▶
MP4 video
📸

Perspective View Support

Animate both orthographic and perspective views. CutScene automatically manages focal lengths, crop boundaries, and centering for seamless reveals.

🔃

Inverse Reveal

Flip any animation with a single checkbox. Show the building dissolving instead of appearing — perfect for demolition or phasing sequences.

📐

Up to 8K Resolution

Export frames from HD to 8K (7680 px). Lock the 16:9 aspect ratio for broadcast-quality output, or let Revit determine the frame dimensions.

🖼️

Raster Density & DPI Control

Full raster density selection from 72 DPI (Draft/Fast) and 150 DPI (Balanced) to 300 DPI (High Res) and 600 DPI (Ultra Print) for razor-sharp, presentation-grade deliverables.

🛡️

100% Non-Destructive

CutScene restores your 3D view — Section Box, Crop Box, and all visibility settings — to its exact original state after every export. Zero side effects.

Workflow

Ready in under 5 minutes

No setup, no configuration - just open Revit and go.

1

Open a 3D View

Enable the Section Box in any Revit 3D view from the Properties panel.

2

Launch CutScene

Click the CutScene button in your Revit Add-Ins ribbon tab.

3

Configure & Export

Pick your reveal mode, direction, resolution, and output format. Click Export.

4

Done!

Your MP4 / GIF lands in the output folder. Your model is fully restored.

Privacy Policy

CutScene by ScanBIM is committed to respecting your privacy. The tool operates locally on your machine within Autodesk Revit and does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, project files, or telemetry to external servers.

Local Processing: All image rendering, frame extraction, and video/GIF compilations (via FFmpeg) are executed strictly on your local computer. No models or rendered frames are ever uploaded or sent over the internet.
